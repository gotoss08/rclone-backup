rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a private OAuth client for the open-source tool rclone. Its owner uses it to upload backup archives from their own servers to their own Google Drive.

It is not a public service: only the owner's Google account is authorized, and there is no sign-up.

The client requests access to Google Drive solely to create, list, update and delete backup files in the owner's Drive.
